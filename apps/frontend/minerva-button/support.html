Report Phishing button

The Report Phishing button lets you flag suspicious or unwanted emails directly from your inbox, so our security team can investigate and protect the rest of the organization.

Where to find it

Open the suspicious email. In the message toolbar, click Report. If you don't see it, it may need to be enabled by your IT admin — contact your helpdesk.

How reporting works

1

Click Report, then choose Suspicious for phishing, impersonation, or malicious links/attachments, or Spam for unwanted marketing.

2

The message is removed from your inbox and sent to our security team for analysis, along with its full headers and content.

3

If it's confirmed malicious, matching copies are automatically removed from every mailbox in the organization.

Frequently asked questions

Will I be notified about the outcome?
Yes. You'll get a short summary once our security team has finished reviewing the message.
I reported an email by mistake — can I undo it?
Reported messages are moved, not deleted. Contact your helpdesk and we can restore it to your inbox.
Does reporting affect the sender?
Confirmed phishing senders are blocked organization-wide. Spam reports only affect filtering, not the sender directly.
